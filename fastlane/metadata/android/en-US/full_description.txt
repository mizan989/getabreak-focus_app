Most digital detox apps want you to sign a contract with yourself: no phone for X minutes, no more than Z minutes in app Y. getabreak works the other way round. It strips the attention-grabbing tricks out of the phone, making calm the default. The protection is simply on, and you pause it mindfully when you need something.

Use your phone. Don't let your phone use you.

HOW IT WORKS

Sort apps and websites into custom groups. A group defines:
* Schedule: always, at night, or during work hours.
* Budget: shared time limit per day or clock hour.
* Action: wait before opening, grayscale, doomscroll break, or lock.
Pre-made templates get you started, and everything is customizable.

WHAT A GROUP CAN DO

* Grayscale, Dim & Blur: Drains color from apps and websites in the group, immediately or once part of the budget is used. On Android 12+, blur turns feeds into soft shapes, discouraging endless scrolling. Works out of the box with zero permissions (for true system grayscale, see SETUP).
* Break Doomscrolling: Detects endless feeds and counts short videos swiped on Reels, Shorts, and TikTok. Enforces a break, followed by a cooldown lock.
* Wait and Lock: Add a short delay before an app opens, show a blocking screen, or deactivate apps and their notifications. Once a budget is spent, apps stay locked until the next window.
* Website Filtering: In Chrome, Firefox, and Chromium/Gecko browsers. Block custom URLs or preset lists for social media, adult content, gambling, dating, shopping, and video.

AND AROUND IT

* Emergency Brake: One tap puts every group in its strictest mode: full screen gray, locked apps stay locked, and schedules/pauses are disabled. Exiting early requires a 10-minute wait.
* Commitment Password: Lock groups behind a password, individually or together. Keep it yourself or give it to an accountability partner.
* Automatic Do Not Disturb: Active while getabreak runs, holding notifications until you are ready.
* Pause Button: Quick Settings tile, assistant, or 2-second hardware button press. Resumes automatically, with a mandatory cooldown between pauses.
* Screen Time & Launcher Widget: Track app/website time, opens, and scroll distance via widgets and notifications. Includes a minimalist text launcher widget to bypass muscle-memory tapping.

SETUP (Optional Hardware Grayscale)

Granting WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS unlocks real system-level grayscale and Extra Dim (below normal minimum brightness). Setup via:
1. Computer using ADB.
2. Directly on-device via Shizuku (guided in-app).
3. Root in one tap.
Survives reboots. Grayscale, Extra Dim, and screen filter work independently. Blur requires Android 12+ (paused during battery saver).

KEEPING IT RUNNING

Aggressive battery managers can stop background apps. getabreak includes keep-alive settings and alerts you if the service is stopped.
